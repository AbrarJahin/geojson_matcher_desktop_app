## Exact production behavior and minimal code map [CODE]
- `app/core/pipeline.py`: `PipelineConfig` reads two county GeoJSONs, expects unique `OBJECTID` IDs, defaults to **50 m** candidate search, projects to **EPSG:26916**; `SAFE_REJECT_GLOBAL_THRESHOLD = 0.937979492358832`. File/input/algorithm fingerprints, decision audits and session recovery exist.
- `app/core/legacy_program.py` is the embedded, retained analytical notebook: `notebook_cell_14.py` loads/projects input; `16` generates spatial-index/radius candidates; `20` engineers geometric, topology and attribute/text evidence; `24/26` build geometry rules plus unsupervised two-component GMM (rule/GMM weighting 0.70/0.30); `28` uses an unsupervised two-class Beta latent-mixture model for textual evidence; `32_safe_reject` fuses scores and classifies; `34_two_category_plan` constructs the review/audit table. Check exact embedded cell names before editing any reference.
- Fusion uses geometric/textual estimates, a data-derived prior and variance weighting, **then batch-wide min–max rescales** the resulting score into column `probablity` (production spelling). These are scoring quantities, **not established calibrated probabilities of truth**. Batch rescaling may change a pair's score when the surrounding candidate set changes; test this experimentally, do not assume robustness across counties.
- Reject iff `probablity < 0.937979492358832` **and** not safeguarded by high direct agreement (geometry rule >= 0.75, textual score >= 0.75, absolute disagreement <= 0.10). Otherwise `MANUAL_REVIEW`. No automatic `YES` acceptance. Every non-rejected candidate is queued; **the obsolete 2%–30% manual-review cap is not enforced**.
- `tests/test_review_policy.py`, `tests/test_pipeline_smoke.py`, `tests/test_pipeline_units.py`: implementation/synthetic checks, **not** independent empirical research validation. `app/core/junctions.py`: subsequent expert-approved junction proposals/geometry edits; downstream routing/network impact belongs to **Paper 2**, not Paper 1.

## Claims, unknowns and paper-ready definition
- Balanced pilot **cannot estimate real-world invalid prevalence or overall rejection precision without sampling design/weights**. Threshold development on those labels invalidates treating its observed zero false negatives as unbiased held-out safety performance. Samples of ~100 do **not** substantiate a 1% upper error bound; roughly 300 *independent, error-free, representative* observations would be needed for a one-sided 95% bound near 1% in a simple binomial setting, and spatial dependence may require more.
